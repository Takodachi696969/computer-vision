The lab's modified MuJoCo model is generated by `simulation/model.py`.
That conversion was added on 2026-10-04 and is distributed under GPL-3.0:

- Preserves each original joint origin, rotation, axis, native radian range,
  link mass, centre of mass, and all six inertia-tensor coefficients.
- Preserves visual mesh geometry, metre scale, and original mesh origins.
- Adds position servos with explicit gains and damping, numerical joint
  armature/damping, gravity, floor contact, free cubes, and a camera.
- Adds a `tcp` site at the bare L6 flange origin, matching the SDK's `NONE`
  tool transform. No gripper or physical grasp mechanism is modeled.
- Uses documented primitive contact proxies in `_COLLISIONS` instead of
  treating concave STLs as accurate collision geometry. Robot/environment,
  cube/cube, and cube/floor contacts are active. Robot self-contact is disabled
  because the inflated proxy bodies overlap around the wrist and joints.
  `world.render(show_collision=True)` makes these contact proxies inspectable.
  Cubes use six-dimensional contact and a higher material priority than the
  floor/robot so their configured sliding, torsional and rolling friction apply.
- Does not model gearboxes, encoder quantization, backlash, stepper current,
  motor speed limits, firmware, cable flex, or controller trajectory timing.
  Joint target limits are checked, but large target jumps are accepted by
  this research simulator. A deployment policy needs a rate/trajectory layer.

The original inertial parameters are **not independently calibrated**.
For example, the L1 centre-of-mass z coordinate is about +0.099m while its
visual mesh z bounds are approximately [-0.049,+0.037]m. L4 has a centre-of-mass
z near -0.092m while its visual bounds are [-0.028,+0.166]m. The converter
retains these source values for traceability. Validate or replace them using
CAD or physical measurement before claiming accurate dynamics or sim-to-real
transfer. The environment is appropriate for learning the interfaces and
developing rigid-body simulation/control workflows.

MuJoCo actuator/integrator/contact configuration follows the official XML
reference: <https://mujoco.readthedocs.io/en/stable/XMLreference.html>.
